# Welcome

I am Md. Dilshadul Alam Maruf, a Mechanical Engineering graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh. My academic and research interests lie at the intersection of materials engineering, electrochemical energy storage, and advanced functional materials.

My long-term research goal is to contribute to the development of next-generation energy-storage technologies through experimental materials synthesis, electrode fabrication, characterization, and electrochemical performance evaluation. I am particularly interested in understanding how material composition, microstructure, surface properties, and electrode–electrolyte interactions influence battery performance, stability, and durability.

With a foundation in mechanical engineering, composite materials, numerical analysis, and industrial engineering, I aim to pursue doctoral research that bridges materials development with practical energy-storage applications.

## Research Interests

My primary research interests focus on experimental development and optimization of advanced electrode materials for rechargeable batteries and supercapacitors.

* **Lithium-ion batteries:** Electrode materials, electrochemical performance, cycling stability, degradation mechanisms, and structure–property relationships.
* **Lithium–sulfur batteries:** Sulfur-based cathodes, conductive host materials, polysulfide confinement, and strategies to improve cycling performance.
* **MXene-based energy-storage materials:** Synthesis, surface and structural modification, electrode fabrication, and the investigation of MXenes for batteries and supercapacitors.
* **Solid-state and beyond-lithium batteries:** Advanced electrode materials and interfaces for emerging sodium-ion, zinc-ion, and solid-state battery systems.
* **Supercapacitors and hybrid energy storage:** Functional electrode materials, charge-storage mechanisms, rate capability, and electrochemical stability.
* **Materials characterization and performance optimization:** Understanding the relationships between composition, microstructure, material properties, and electrochemical behavior.

I am especially interested in research that combines experimental investigation with a fundamental understanding of material properties and electrochemical mechanisms. Computational analysis and machine learning may serve as complementary tools for interpreting experimental results and guiding materials optimization.

## Research Background

### Undergraduate Thesis: Composite Materials for Brake-Pad Applications

My undergraduate thesis investigated the development and evaluation of rice-husk- and coconut-shell-based composite materials reinforced with silicon carbide (SiC) for brake-pad applications.

The work involved investigating different constituent ratios, including formulations based on rice husk, coconut shell, and SiC, to understand their influence on material properties and performance. The study strengthened my interest in composite development, materials engineering, performance evaluation, and the relationship between material composition and functional properties.

This experience provided a foundation for approaching materials research through systematic formulation, comparative evaluation, and engineering analysis. Although my undergraduate thesis focused on tribological composite materials rather than electrochemical systems, it helped develop my broader interest in designing and optimizing functional materials for demanding applications.

### Research on MXene Materials for Energy Storage

I have contributed to research on MXene materials and their potential applications in energy storage and power systems. This research aligns with my growing interest in two-dimensional materials, their tunable properties, and their potential to enable improved electrochemical performance.

My related publication is titled *State-of-the-Art Advancement in MXene For Energy Storage and Power System Applications*. Through this research, I have developed a stronger interest in MXene-based electrodes, advanced battery architectures, and the opportunities for designing materials with improved conductivity, electrochemical activity, and stability.

I am interested in extending this research direction toward experimental investigation, particularly the synthesis and modification of electrode materials and the evaluation of their electrochemical properties.

I have also been involved in additional research work, including a manuscript currently under review.
